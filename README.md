### About

Accounting and finance background, now in software quality assurance.

I spent six years preparing and filing tax returns, reconciling bank statements, and
auditing more than 40 branches against internal standards, which is where I learned to
find the one number that is wrong before anyone else notices. After a software
engineering bootcamp I do the same thing to software.

I test financial applications the way an accountant reads a ledger: I know what the
numbers are supposed to do, then I prove whether the application actually does it.

For roles and full career history → [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/in/gamzesimit) or [email](mailto:gamzenursimit@gmail.com).
